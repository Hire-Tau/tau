Tau brings conversations, work streams, and teams of agents into one workspace. Use the Assistant to find work, explore an idea, or ask a squad to produce something. Follow the result in the Feed and step in when a decision needs you.

## Choose your setup

Choose the instructions for your workspace. You can change this selection at any time beside the theme control, or in the menu on mobile. Most of Tau works the same in both modes.

## Complete your first task

The [first-task walkthrough](/start/first-task/) takes you from a working instance to a finished, written deliverable. It uses a small example that needs no repository or chat integration.

You will create a squad, choose a workflow, start a conversation through the Assistant, and check the completed result.

## Use the Assistant

Open the Assistant with **⌘K** on macOS or **Ctrl K** on Windows and Linux. Search for a squad, work stream, or conversation; open it inline; or ask Tau for help.

[Find work, ask questions, and use live voice →](/use/assistant/)

## Work with your agents

| What you want to do                                | Guide                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Give a team a purpose, workspace, and instructions | [Create and organize squads](/use/squads/)         |

## Connect your tools

Start with a [model provider](/configure/models/). Add [GitHub](/connect/github/), [project tools](/connect/project-tools/), or [chat channels](/connect/chat/) when your tasks need them. Set up [notifications](/connect/notifications/) so you know when work needs you.

## Run and maintain Tau

Self-hosted administrators can choose a [runtime and access setup](/self-host/runtime-and-access/), plan [updates and backups](/self-host/maintenance/), and [troubleshoot problems](/self-host/troubleshooting/).

For exact commands and configuration locations, see the [CLI](/reference/cli/) and [configuration reference](/reference/configuration/).
